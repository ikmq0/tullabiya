# Batch 1: favorites that work, your color, digest, video, rail, stamps

Owner's brief (3 Nov 2026): make favorites do something, add a video news story, fix the profile
popover (phone and laptop), drop the college step, follow publishers instead and filter by them, a
real 5-minute version, a ViBook note on the calendar, an edition rail with minutes left, plus the
no-backend ideas from the brainstorm: weekly clue drops, "since your last visit", stamps, a first-read
nudge. Everything stays on the local simulation (`LocalApi`).

## Decisions

- **Your color replaces the college.** `Profile.college` becomes `Profile.color: AccentId` (the same
  seven brand accents, named by hue). Stored profiles migrate in `LocalApi.getProfile` (college id ->
  accent id, same index). The poll "your college vs. all" line goes; the breakdown data stays in the
  Api for the real backend.
- **Follows drive "for you".** `Favorites.units` (publishers) + `Favorites.sections`. An event is for
  you when its `unitId` is followed; a story when any of its `unitIds` is followed or its section is
  pinned. The strip's highlighted bars, the hero count, the favorites band and the filter chip all use
  `isForMe` in `src/lib/personalization.ts`. Event `colleges` targeting stays in the data, unused.
- **Filter chip** ("مفضلاتي فقط") is one island (`ForYouFilter`) that toggles a persisted
  `$forYou` atom; server-rendered lists carry `data-section` / `data-units` and hide non-matching rows
  with a CSS attribute; `CalendarView` reads the atom directly.
- **Follow where you meet them:** `EntityChip` for a unit gets a star toggle.
- **5-minute version** is a page, `/[lang]/quick/`: one slab per section (pinned first), one action
  each, a running "N min left"; reaching the end sets `progress.digestDone` and earns a stamp.
- **Video story:** `Story.video = { youtube, minutes }`; `LiteVideo` shows a local poster cut at 5°
  with a play mark and swaps in a `youtube-nocookie` iframe on tap. New story `21-research-day`
  (sample text) is a news side card.
- **Edition rail:** `EditionRail` island on the home; sections as notches, label with the section
  name, minutes left in the issue and in the section; shown while scrolling, fades when idle.
  Minutes per section come from story `readingMinutes` plus fixed values for interactive blocks.
- **Weekly clue drops:** clue k unlocks on days 1, 8, 15 of the issue month by the demo clock
  (`cluesOpenOn`), attempts no longer unlock clues; the block says when the next clue drops.
- **Since your last visit:** `LocalApi` keeps `lastVisit` {at, pollTotals, clueIndex}; `hydrate`
  computes deltas once per visit and the hero shows one line when there is something to say.
- **Stamps:** global `stamps: {id, at}[]` in the simulation, earned on first vote, first solve,
  five follows, both languages seen, digest done, three issues in a row. A toast announces one;
  My Corner shows them as octagons in your color.
- **Nudge:** the onboarding sheet opens by itself once, after the first story is read or the first
  vote, when there is no profile.
- **Popover fix:** the end-of-issue `.end` styles leaked into `.popover.end`; the popover alignment
  class is now `pop-end`, and the popover sizes to its content within the viewport.
